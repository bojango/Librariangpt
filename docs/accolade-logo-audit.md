# Accolade logo provenance audit

Audited 2026-09-19 against the complete live `public.accolades` catalogue. Statuses are:

- **A** — official/reliable mark installed.
- **B** — an official mark appears to exist, but no safe stable asset could be retrieved.
- **C** — no distinct appropriate visual mark was found.
- **D** — licensing, rights, or brand ambiguity requires a manual decision.

Installed files are normalized WebP derivatives that preserve the source artwork and proportions. Their versioned names must change if their contents are materially replaced.
